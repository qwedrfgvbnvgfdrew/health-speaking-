1. Grammar

Three structures from Days 6, 8 and 14. Use each one in every answer.

Day 6: opinion

Full sentence + which I believe + is + adj

Adds your view after any fact.

Not: “which I believe it is”.

Day 8: not yet

Subject + to be + yet to + verb

Something has not happened, but will.

Verb stays in the base form.

Day 14: almost zero

little to no + noun

Works with time, money, effort, chance.

Means “very little, nearly none”.

My city has a health problem, which I believe is serious. Many people do little to no exercise, so they feel tired. The new park is yet to open, which I think is a pity, because families have little to no safe places to walk. Our hospital is yet to hire enough doctors, which I believe is worrying. Still, young people run together every morning, which I think is wonderful. Little to no change happens overnight, yet every step counts.
